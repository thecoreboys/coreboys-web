# Guide layout follow-up

The Guide page and Theater guide share refinements in `app/guide-polish.css`, loaded by the app shell. The page keeps filters within the viewport, gives the member selector a full row on small phones, removes duplicate dropdown chevrons, and wraps timeline controls with 44px zoom buttons. Timeline scrolling remains local, with a subtle scrollbar.

Both surfaces open with Now & next. Channel cards show artwork, live/replay labels, progress through a scheduled replay, a watch action, and the following program. Full schedule retains the desktop timeline and phone agenda. Search remains visible on phones; expanded filters have a clear action. Phone date navigation is independent of the desktop scroller, and returning to Now & next after browsing history requests current airings again.

The player guide now opens the same `GuideGrid` used by `/guide` in a full-page Radix dialog. It covers the player and its sidebar, traps focus, scrolls independently, and provides a Back to media player button. Back and Escape preserve the existing player; choosing a playable program closes the guide and tunes it. The old separate compact player timeline is no longer rendered.

Below 768px, Full schedule displays chronological cards in normal document flow. Titles wrap fully without line clamps, timestamps remain explicit, and Show more reveals additional programs per channel. Now & next titles also wrap fully. Desktop timeline cards have more room, do not scale across neighboring cards on hover, and use a tested packing helper that adds tiers at the timeline edge rather than clamping cards on top of one another. True duration geometry for the continuous desktop timeline is retained.

The initial server snapshot retains available replay channels while the archive loads. The guide API bounds archive waiting to eight seconds, then allows four seconds for its recent-catalog fallback. Slow optional sources return `loading`, keep their shared request in flight, and are polled every five seconds until settled. Successful provider data and recorded history are cached, including results completed after the response deadline. Actual failures remain distinguishable from pending data, preserve available programming, and use normal retry timing. Pending and failed responses are not CDN cached. The player requests a bounded date window instead of downloading the complete archive before opening.

Verification includes the full TypeScript check, lint, 55 focused guide tests, dense-card placement invariants, and browser checks for phone filters, date navigation, light appearance, and returning/tuning from the player guide. Current preview screenshots are in `output/guide-refresh/`; earlier geometry records are in `output/guide-ui/`.
