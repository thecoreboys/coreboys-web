## What the system does

The CORE face-presence pilot creates numerical face templates from the enrollment images I provide. For a source covered by this consent, the system may create a temporary numerical template for a visible face and compare it only with the enrolled, consented allowlist. A staff member reviews suggested time ranges before any public tag is shown.

The system is not used for authentication, security, law enforcement, moderation punishment, eligibility, emotion analysis, demographic inference, or identifying people who are not enrolled.

## Data and retention

Enrollment images are protected from public access. A pending upload expires within 7 days if independent quality review does not complete. Rejected images are deleted immediately. An accepted raw enrollment upload receives a fresh retention deadline and is deleted within 24 hours after quality review (or sooner when consent expires). Review evidence is normally deleted within 7 days after a decision, diagnostic logs within 30 days, and active templates require affirmative renewal after 12 months. Unknown face data is not intentionally persisted.

The site does not sell, lease, trade, or profit from a face template. Public clients receive only reviewed identity/profile/time-range metadata, not face templates, private reference images, evidence crops, raw similarity, or model internals.

## Revocation, correction, and deletion

I may revoke one or more purposes or request correction/deletion by contacting **privacy@thecoreboys.com**. Matching and new public tags for the revoked purpose will be disabled promptly. The operator will delete covered enrollment images, templates, derived identity prototypes, review evidence, and related caches according to the verified deletion process and bounded backup schedule, subject only to records the law requires to be retained.
