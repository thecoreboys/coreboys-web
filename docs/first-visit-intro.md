# First Home visit intro

`FirstVisitIntro` keeps Home mounted behind a full-screen native dialog and runs in four parts.

1. **Boot terminal.** A retro console ("Preparing your CORE experience") reports the real preload: viewport, intro video bytes (streamed from the response body), a decode check from the actual `<video>` element, deferred Home shelves staged, shelf artwork warmed four at a time, and the six cast portraits. The bar is a weighted view of those parts (`introPreloadPercent`) and reads 100% only when every part is ready. It stays up for at least `INTRO_START_DELAY_MS`. A missing portrait never holds it longer than `INTRO_PORTRAIT_GRACE_MS`, and slow artwork keeps loading during playback.
2. **Begin gate.** "Press Enter to begin · Sound on" (touch: "Tap to begin") is the gesture that lets the browser play with sound. `play()` runs inside that click or key handler. A press during boot queues the start. If the browser still refuses sound, playback starts muted and a "Turn sound on" prompt stays over the video. "Watch without sound" and "Skip intro" are always available.
3. **Stage.** The 1024×420 video gets the largest cinemascope frame that fits. As each friend is named, a card with their portrait, number and community rises from the top or bottom edge of the frame. On phones the cards sit above and below an edge-to-edge frame, and in landscape they sit inside it. Cue timings live in one list, `INTRO_NAME_CUES` in `lib/first-visit-intro.ts`. They were measured from the on-screen name titles: JasonTheWeen 13.2s, StableRonaldo 16.2s, Lacy 20.0s, Adapt 25.5s, Silky 29.5s and Marlon 34.0s. Names, portraits and accent colours come from `lib/members.ts`. The footer has pause, restart and sound controls, a timeline with cue ticks, and K / M / Esc shortcuts. A live region announces each name.
4. **Credits and exit.** Credits list the cast, the intro edit (`@underscoreemmaa`), the site builder (`@berryeyu`) and the fan-site disclaimer, with Replay and Enter CORE. After `INTRO_CREDITS_MS` the screen powers down to a line, a seam flares, and the console parts to reveal Home. The hero player waits until the dialog has fully closed.

Actual playback or an explicit skip records `coreboys:welcome-seen=1` in local storage, with a `core_welcome_seen` cookie fallback. Remembered visitors download nothing. Add `?intro=1` to the Home URL to show the intro again. A failed download can be retried without marking the intro as seen. Existing main-player sessions stay in control.

The OS reduced-motion setting, the in-app calm or reduced-motion modes, and the conserve device profile (`prefersMinimalMotion`) remove the typing, power and shutter effects. Cards then simply appear, and the exit is a short fade. Curves come from the shared `--motion-*` tokens in `app/motion.css`.

The 45.7-second clip is served as `/intro/core-welcome-v1.mp4` (H.264/AAC, front-loaded index, 8,395,517 bytes). Browser policy references: [Chrome autoplay](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/autoplay/) and [WebKit inline video](https://webkit.org/blog/6784/new-video-policies-for-ios/).
